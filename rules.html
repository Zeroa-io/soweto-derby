Competition rules

Soweto Derby score prediction, 31 October 2026. Version SOWETO-v7, 26 October 2026.

1. The promoter

This competition is run by Chembridge Capital (PTY) Ltd t/a Zeroa, registration number 2014/064942/07, 27th Floor, The Box, 9 Lower Burg Street, Cape Town 8001 ("Zeroa"). Questions go to hello@zeroa.io.

2. When it runs

Entries open on Monday 26 October 2026 and close at kick-off, 15:30 South African time on Saturday 31 October 2026. Late entries are not accepted.

3. Who can enter

  1. South African residents aged 18 or older.
  2. Zeroa's directors, members, partners, employees, agents and consultants may not win. Neither may anyone who directly or indirectly controls or is controlled by Zeroa.
  3. Suppliers of goods or services for this competition may not win. This includes the advertising, media and prize suppliers, the overseer in rule 9, and their directors, employees and agents.
  4. The spouses, life partners, business partners and immediate family members of anyone in rules 3.2 and 3.3 may not win.

4. How to enter

  1. On the competition page, predict the full-time score of Orlando Pirates v Kaizer Chiefs. Fill in your first name, last name, email address and South African mobile number. Tick to confirm you are 18 or older and live in South Africa, and tick to accept these rules.
  2. Entry is free. You don't need to buy anything, open a Zeroa account or make a deposit to enter or to win.
  3. One entry per person. If you enter again with the same email address or mobile number, only your first entry counts.
  4. Incomplete, false or automated entries are not valid.

5. Which score counts

  1. The score that counts is the full-time score after 90 minutes plus stoppage time, as published by the Premier Soccer League (PSL). Later disciplinary changes to the result are ignored.
  2. If the match is postponed, or stopped and then finished later or replayed, entries made before the closing time count for the completed match. No new entries are accepted.
  3. If the match is cancelled or not completed by 31 January 2027, the winner is drawn at random from all valid entries. The same applies if the PSL decides the result without the match being completed.

6. How the winner is picked

  1. One winner is drawn at random from the valid entries with the correct score.
  2. If no valid entry has the correct score, the winner is drawn at random from the valid entries with the correct result (a Pirates win, a Chiefs win or a drawn match).
  3. If no valid entry has the correct result, the winner is drawn at random from all valid entries.
  4. The draw takes place by Monday 9 November 2026. If rule 5.2 applies, the draw takes place within 5 business days after the match is completed. If it is not completed by 31 January 2027, the draw takes place within 5 business days after that date.

7. The prize

  1. The prize is 5 tickets in the most expensive seats available for the PSL match Kaizer Chiefs v Orlando Pirates on Saturday 13 March 2027 at FNB Stadium, Johannesburg. If the PSL changes the date or venue, the tickets are for the new date and venue.
  2. Zeroa sends the tickets to the winner by email, WhatsApp or courier, at no cost to the winner, no later than 17:00 on the day before the match.
  3. If that match is cancelled or Zeroa cannot get the tickets, Zeroa tells the winner. Within 10 business days after that, Zeroa pays the winner the official price of 5 tickets in those seats into a South African bank account in the winner's name. If no price was published, the price of those seats at the most recent Soweto Derby at FNB Stadium is used.

8. Contacting the winner

  1. Zeroa contacts the winner by email and WhatsApp within 3 business days of the draw. If there is no reply, Zeroa tries at least 2 more times on different business days. If the winner has not replied within 5 business days of the first contact, a new winner is drawn under rule 6 from the remaining valid entries, and the same steps apply.
  2. The winner must show a South African ID document or passport, and sign to confirm they received the prize.
  3. The winner does not have to take part in any marketing, allow their image to be used or attend the draw. Zeroa only publishes the winner's name or photo with their consent.
  4. The result is posted on the competition page by the draw date in rule 6.4. It shows the official score and that a winner has been drawn.

9. Independent oversight

An accountant or registered auditor who is independent of Zeroa oversees and certifies the competition and the draw, as regulation 11(5) under the Consumer Protection Act requires.

10. Your personal information

  1. Zeroa uses your details to run the competition and contact the winner, and keeps them for 3 years as the Consumer Protection Act requires. If you came from a partner's link or QR code, Zeroa records that partner's code with your entry to credit them. All fields on the entry form are needed to enter. Entries from anyone under 18 are deleted.
  2. Your entry is stored with Zoho, which hosts Zeroa's forms and customer records. Zoho keeps it in the United States, and Zoho companies in other countries may process it to support the service. The United States has no data protection law like POPIA, so your details may have less legal protection there. By accepting these rules, you agree to your details being stored and processed outside South Africa. Zeroa also shares the entries with the overseer in rule 9 to certify the draw.
  3. You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, object to their use, withdraw your consent and complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za). The full privacy policy explains how.

  2. This competition is not affiliated with or endorsed by the PSL, Orlando Pirates, Kaizer Chiefs or the South African Football Association.
  3. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding South African public holidays.
